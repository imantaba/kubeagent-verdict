# kubeagent-verdict — Project Notes for Claude

## Writing style — simple voice (always)

Every README, doc, runbook section, report, and chat summary for this
project is written in **simple voice**:

- Short sentences. Everyday words. Explain any jargon in one line, where
  it first appears.
- Lead with the thing the reader must decide or do. Background comes after.
- When presenting options, give each one: what it costs, what you learn,
  and its downside — then a clear recommendation. A small table helps.
- Numbers carry their meaning in words: "3 of 10 pairs", not just "0.3".
- Say costs and risks plainly: "this takes 18 hours and may fix nothing."

The bar: someone new to the project understands the text on first read,
without opening another file.
